# Rolling

The scroll icon in the bar above the map opens the **rolling cheat sheet**: the jumps that close a wormhole or leave it critical with one ship. Pick the hole's mass from the list on the left, or type its code to find it.

A hole's real mass is its type's give or take 10%, and the game does not say which. So the sheet has you put about half the type's mass through first and then look at the hole:

- **Reduced** means more than half is gone, so the hole is on the small side.
- **Not reduced** means it is on the big side.

Each answer has its own jumps **to roll** (close the hole) and **to crit** (leave it open and nearly gone). Every set ends with the roller at home, and none of them can close the hole while the roller is on the far side.

## Your ship

At the bottom, type in the rolling ship's mass with its propulsion module off (**cold**) and on (**hot**), in kt. The sheet is worked out for that ship and remembers it. It starts with a battleship of 200 and 300 kt.

Holes the ship does not fit through are at the end of the list.

## When it cannot be sure

Sometimes no number of jumps closes the hole for certain without risking the roller. The sheet then stops at what is safe and says the hole can stay open, critical where that much is certain. Finish it with something light, such as a heavy interdictor.

The sheet assumes a hole nothing has gone through. For a hole that already has mass on it, treat it as reduced and keep something light at hand.
